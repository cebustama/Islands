## Proposed analysis (for Islands PCG and Noise)

Goal: recover **techniques, algorithms, parameters and ideas** that Islands can use, not a wrapped of the whole collection. Groups C–H only feed Islands where they give a natural rule (rivers, rainforests, islands); the rest is routed to other projects as one-line links.

1. **Triage (done above).** Tier 1: group A (23 talks/breakdowns, of which ~11 are [new]). Tier 2: groups B and D. Tier 3: C (rules from nature). E–H: not analysed for Islands.
2. **Transcripts in Chrome**, as in `marcadores-chrome/profundo/fichas_videos.md`: read, write our own summary, **do not save the transcript text**. Videos without subtitles: title, description and chapters, and say so.
3. **One card per video**, fixed schema:
   - techniques, mapped to `PCG Techniques/pcg_technique_library.md` cards (D.1–D.20) or to the web catalog (`web/.../src/data/islands-techniques.js`), new ones flagged;
   - the algorithm in steps, parameters and their ranges, determinism/seed handling, chunk/seam behaviour;
   - minute marks;
   - Islands status: exists / partial / missing (checked against code, not against `technique_integration_matrix.md`, which has known drift);
   - where it would land: stage of the pipeline, `Runtime/Noise`, NoiseLab, an Islands Lab page.
4. **Cross-reference** technique × videos × Islands status → list of gaps and confirmations.
5. **Ranked backlog** of candidates (effort, value, which phase), for Claudio to decide. Nothing goes into the roadmap without his OK.

Output, inside this folder: `playlist-pcg/` with `cards.md`, `crossref.md`, `backlog.md` (and optionally a wrapped).
