## What the plates show

- Chladni held a metal or glass plate (by its centre, or with fingers on the edge), sprinkled sand on it and bowed the edge. At each resonant frequency the plate vibrates in a **standing wave (mode)**: some regions move a lot (antinodes), some lines do not move at all (**nodal lines**). Sand bounces off the moving parts and settles on the still lines. One figure = one note; higher notes = more lines.
- Mode selection: a finger on the edge forces a node there; the bow forces an antinode where it touches.
- **Circular plates (Tab. I–IV):** each mode has *n* nodal diameters and *m* nodal circles (figs 18–24, 39). Radial shape J_n(k·r), angular cos(nθ).
- **Square plates (Tab. IX–X):** two modes with the same frequency mix: cos(nπx)cos(mπy) ± cos(mπx)cos(nπy). The sign/mix explains the diagonals and curves (figs 107, 114, 138). This is the membrane (Laplacian) approximation; the real plate obeys Kirchhoff's biharmonic equation ∇⁴w = λw, which has no closed form for a free square plate (Ritz, 1909, solved it numerically).
- **Black stars (figs 6, 8, 11–13)** — *inferred, not from a source:* near the centre the amplitude grows like rⁿ, so with many diameters the centre barely moves and sand fills a star.
- **Inverted figures (3, 5)** — *probable:* fine powder (not sand) gathers at the antinodes; Faraday explained it in 1831 by air currents.
- **Chladni's law** for circular plates: f ≈ C·(m + 2n)^p, p ≈ 2 for flat plates.

## Can Islands do it?

Yes, cheaply.

1. **Analytic field.** A Chladni figure is a scalar field f(x,y), like a noise. Nodal lines = |f| < ε, the same mechanism as water-by-threshold and the contour layer in NoiseLab. Cosines (square) or Bessel × cosine (circle) are deterministic and Burst-friendly. Uniform line width: |f| / |∇f| < w.
2. **Exact physics** of a free plate needs a numerical eigen-solver for ∇⁴. Islands has none; the v6 pressure projection solves Poisson, a different problem. Could be done offline and baked.
3. **Sand as particles:** a random walk whose step is proportional to |f| at the grain's position. Grains diffuse fast where the plate moves and get stuck where it does not — the physical mechanism, and the same family as density sampling in [`2026-10-31-hydrogen-cloud-sampling.md`](2026-10-31-hydrogen-cloud-sampling.md). Plate modes are the 2D analogue of orbital eigenstates.

## History (efecto Matilda)

After Chladni's demonstration in Paris (1808), Napoleon backed a prize of the Academy of Sciences to explain the figures. **Sophie Germain** entered in 1811 (no prize), again, and won on the third attempt (January 1816), the first woman to win a prize of the Paris Academy. The judges noted shortcomings (she relied on an incorrect equation from Euler); Kirchhoff fixed the boundary conditions in 1850 and history remembers him. She had earlier corresponded with Gauss as «M. Le Blanc». Sources checked by search on 2026-11-19: [APS News](https://aps.org/publications/apsnews/200405/history.cfm), [Agnes Scott, Biographies of Women Mathematicians](https://www.agnesscott.edu/LRiddle/women/germain.htm); exact date and prize amount differ between sources. Book: Bucciarelli & Dworsky, *Sophie Germain: An Essay in the History of the Theory of Elasticity* (Reidel, 1980).

## The experiment at home (2026-11-19, Claudio asked)

- **Bow (Chladni's way):** a 20–30 cm metal plate (aluminium or steel, 1–2 mm), bolted at its centre to a clamp; fine dry sand or salt; a rosined violin or cello bow drawn straight down the edge. A finger on the edge forces a node; the bow point is an antinode. Changing both selects the figure.
- **Speaker:** a phone tone generator → amplifier → speaker or wave driver, with the plate bolted to the cone centre (cover the cone against the sand). Sweep the frequency slowly: the figure appears only near a resonance. Keep the volume low and protect your ears.
- **Membrane (cheapest):** a balloon or plastic film stretched over a bowl on top of a speaker. This is a membrane, not a plate (Watts Hughes' eidophone).

## Why the chords do not come out in tune

The plate offers a fixed set of frequencies set by its shape, thickness and material. Their ratios are not whole multiples: the overtones are inharmonic, as in bells and metallophones. Equal temperament asks for irrational ratios (2^(k/12)), so the page takes the nearest mode. In the membrane model the square plate has f ∝ n²+m²: 16:20 gives an exact 5:4, but many intervals have no mode, with n and m ≤ 10. A real plate also deviates from the membrane model. Link: Sethares, *Tuning, Timbre, Spectrum, Scale* (consonance depends on the spectrum; gamelan tunings fit metallophone spectra).
